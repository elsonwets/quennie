# Quennie media
Eight public TikTok publications are recorded in src/quennie-media.json with original source URLs.
4 MP4 videos downloaded with yt-dlp. 25 album images downloaded with gallery-dl. Video posters extracted with ffmpeg (imageio-ffmpeg package); album photos and posters optimized to WebP with Pillow.
Public assets: public/media. Raw album downloads: .media-source (ignored by git).
Videos keep native playback controls and load on user request. No TikTok login or browser cookies are used by the site. Public source availability can change.
The color atelier is a preference palette, not a verified inventory of fabrics. Choices, favorites and measurements travel into the quote and WhatsApp message.
